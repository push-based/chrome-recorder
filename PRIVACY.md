# Chrome Recorder privacy policy

_Last updated: 19 November 2026_

Chrome Recorder is a Chrome extension by PushBased that records a browser tab,
a window or a screen to a video file. This policy covers the extension and the
data it handles.

## What it handles

Only while you are recording, and only what you chose to record:

- **This tab:** the pixels and, if you turned audio on, the sound of the active
  tab, captured by Chrome's built-in recorder through the `debugger`
  permission. The extension uses that permission only to start and stop the
  recording; it doesn't read or change the page.
- **Screen or window:** the screen, window or tab you pick in Chrome's share
  dialog, plus the audio you allow there.

The extension also remembers your last settings (source, frame rate, size,
audio) in the extension's own local storage.

## Where it goes

Nowhere outside your computer. Each recording is saved with Chrome's downloads
feature to the `chrome-recorder` folder in your downloads folder, and a preview
is kept in the side panel's memory until the panel closes. The extension makes
no network requests, has no accounts, and uses no analytics or tracking.

The side panel links to push-based.io. Following that link is an ordinary visit
to that website, under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

Recordings are never uploaded, sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.

## Changes

If the extension starts handling data differently, this page will change first,
with a new date at the top.
